13. Commercial Readiness (Pilot)
To ensure Phase 4 validates not only the financial architecture but also the commercial viability of the platform, implement lightweight Subscription Management and Referral Management modules. These are intended solely to support pilot operations and collect business insights. Advanced marketing automation, CRM, and campaign management remain within the future roadmap.

13.1 Basic Subscription Management
Purpose
Provide a simple subscription framework that allows organisations, landlords, and future partners to subscribe to Secure Living plans, manage billing, and validate the commercial model during the pilot phase.
Scope
The subscription module should support:
Configurable subscription plans (e.g. Self-Management, Professional Management) 
Monthly and annual billing cycles 
Trial period support 
Subscription activation, renewal, suspension, cancellation, and expiry 
Plan upgrades and downgrades 
Subscription status tracking (Trial, Active, Suspended, Cancelled, Expired) 
Billing history 
Renewal reminders 
Basic invoice generation 
Integration with supported payment methods (M-Pesa and configured payment gateways) 
Role-based subscription visibility for Super Admin, Admin, and Organisation owners 
Deliverables
Subscription Plans module 
Organisation Subscription Management 
Billing & Renewal Workflow 
Subscription History 
Plan-based Feature Access Control 
Subscription Reporting Dashboard 

13.2 Basic Referral Programme
Purpose
Provide a simple referral system to encourage early platform adoption while measuring customer acquisition through referrals during the pilot.
Scope
Eligible referrers include:
Landlords 
Property Managers 
Agencies 
Tenants 
Service Providers 
The referral module should support:
Unique referral codes and referral links 
Referral registration tracking 
Referral status tracking 
Qualification rules 
Reward eligibility tracking 
Referral activity history 
Referral Status
Invited 
Registered 
Verified 
Qualified 
Rewarded 
Configurable Rewards
The reward engine should support configurable rewards such as:
Free subscription period 
Account credit 
Listing credit 
Management fee discount 
Promotional voucher 
Note: Cash rewards are not required during the pilot phase unless specifically enabled.
Deliverables
Referral Code Generation 
Referral Tracking Dashboard 
Referral Activity Log 
Referral Reporting 
Reward Approval Workflow 

13.3 Commercial Readiness Metrics
To evaluate the success of the pilot, capture and report the following metrics:
Subscription plan adoption 
Trial-to-paid conversion rate 
Subscription renewal rate 
Referral conversion rate 
Number of successful referrals 
Customer acquisition source (Direct, Referral, Partner) 
Average revenue per organization 
Pilot feedback on pricing and subscription plans
